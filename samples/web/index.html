Scan a document

This page asks the papertrail app on your computer to scan with your TWAIN scanner and sends the pages back here. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Color

How this works
  1. The page talks to papertrail at http://127.0.0.1:47115. Your browser may ask to let this site reach apps on your device. Allow it.
  2. The first time, papertrail asks you to approve this website.
  3. Each scan is confirmed in papertrail before the scanner starts.
  4. If papertrail is not running, the page opens a papertrail:// link to start it.
