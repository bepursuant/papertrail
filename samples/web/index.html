This page asks the papertrail app on your computer to scan with your TWAIN scanner and sends the pages back here. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Provenance stamp

Adds a band with the scan time, computer, Windows user, scanner, page number, this site and a scan id. papertrail fills it in; the page only picks where it goes.

How this works
  1. The page talks to papertrail at http://127.0.0.1:47115. Your browser may ask to let this site reach apps on your device. Allow it.
  2. The first time, papertrail asks you to approve this website. After that, Scan starts the scanner straight away.
  3. If papertrail is not running, the page opens a papertrail:// link to start it.
